# Seller Adoption and Cohort Analytics

## Portfolio objective

Demonstrate longitudinal SQL, cohort design, activation and retention metrics, behavioral segmentation, and BI communication.

## Business problem

A product team needs to understand whether sellers adopt a feature, continue using it, return after inactivity, and behave differently after activation.

## Core metrics

- Eligible sellers
- First-use activation rate
- Weekly and monthly active adoption
- 30/60/90-day retention
- Reactivation rate
- Usage frequency
- Adoption by channel, tenure, volume, and cohort
- Pre/post operational outcomes

## Important design choices

- Use a fixed observation anchor date.
- Separate eligibility from activity.
- Avoid double counting sellers linked to multiple configurations.
- Document cohort inclusion and censoring rules.
- Use weekly panels for trends and monthly panels for stability.
