# Enterprise AI Usage, Adoption & ROI Analytics Platform

## Portfolio objective

Demonstrate analytics engineering, AI product analytics, BI delivery, data-quality
testing, cost measurement, responsible-AI monitoring, and executive communication.

## Business problem

Enterprise leaders need to understand whether licensed employees are adopting
generative AI, which use cases create value, what fixed and variable costs look
like, and where quality, governance, or license-utilization issues need attention.

## Solution

- Eight synthetic raw data sources
- PostgreSQL ingestion and validation
- A 26-model dbt warehouse with 120 automated tests
- Six dashboard-ready marts
- A six-page Streamlit dashboard
- Tableau-ready extracts and documented metric definitions
