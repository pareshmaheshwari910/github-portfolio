# Healthcare Data Quality Analytics

## Portfolio objective

Show healthcare-domain understanding, data profiling, validation rules, issue prioritization, and remediation reporting.

## Business problem

Healthcare analytics depends on trusted provider, patient, claims, and engagement data. Missing, inconsistent, duplicated, or stale records reduce reporting reliability and downstream targeting quality.

## Quality dimensions

- Completeness
- Validity
- Consistency
- Uniqueness
- Timeliness
- Referential integrity

## Suggested entities

Use synthetic versions of:

- Healthcare professional master
- Organization master
- Claims
- CRM activity
- Territory mapping

## Suggested outputs

- Data-quality scorecard
- Issue heat map
- Rule-level failure table
- Trend by source and country
- Remediation queue
- Before/after quality comparison
