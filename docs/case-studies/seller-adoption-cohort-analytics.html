Seller Adoption and Cohort Analytics

A longitudinal framework for measuring activation, ongoing adoption, retention, reactivation, and post-adoption behavior across seller cohorts.

SQL, Python, TableauPrimary tools
Seller-week and seller-monthGrain
Adoption and retentionFocus

The challenge

A single adoption percentage hides important differences between eligibility, initial activation, repeated usage, retention, and reactivation. It can also overcount sellers when one seller is connected to multiple carriers, channels, or technical integrations.

Measurement framework

MetricDefinition
Eligible sellersSellers meeting the product and activity requirements during the observation period
ActivationFirst qualifying use after eligibility
Active adoptionQualifying use during a week or month
RetentionContinued use after activation at defined intervals
ReactivationReturn to use after a defined inactive period

SQL design

  1. Created one canonical seller record for each observation period.
  2. Collapsed multiple carrier and channel relationships into seller-level flags.
  3. Calculated first-use dates using window functions.
  4. Built complete seller-week and seller-month panels to retain inactive periods.
  5. Anchored retention windows to a fixed maximum observation date.
  6. Separated recent cohorts that had not yet completed the full retention window.

Key analytical insight

A seller can be associated with multiple carriers or configurations. Treating each relationship as a separate seller would inflate adoption and distort conversion. The model therefore measures adoption at the seller level while preserving carrier-level attributes for segmentation.

Dashboard design

  • Activation funnel from eligible to first use.
  • Cohort heat map for retention over time.
  • Weekly active adoption trend.
  • Reactivation and churn movement.
  • Segmentation by volume, tenure, channel, and integration type.

Business value

The analysis distinguishes acquisition problems from retention problems. It helps product, sales, and operations teams identify whether they should improve onboarding, expand eligibility, address technical friction, or re-engage previously active sellers.
