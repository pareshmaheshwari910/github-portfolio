Healthcare Data Quality Analytics

A reusable data-quality framework for healthcare professional, claims, CRM activity, and territory data, designed around transparent validation rules and remediation priorities.

SQL, Python, BIPrimary tools
Life sciences and healthcareDomain
Synthetic recordsData

The challenge

Healthcare reporting depends on consistent identities and reference data across provider masters, claims, CRM calls, and territory mappings. Missing identifiers, invalid specialties, duplicate professionals, stale addresses, and broken references can undermine segmentation and downstream reporting.

Quality dimensions

DimensionExample rule
CompletenessRequired identifiers and specialty fields are populated
ValidityValues conform to approved formats and reference lists
ConsistencyProvider attributes agree across master and activity sources
UniquenessDuplicate professionals and organizations are identified
TimelinessRecords meet source-specific freshness expectations
Referential integrityClaims and calls resolve to valid master entities

Solution design

I structured rules as metadata so every check has a source, field, severity, owner, threshold, and remediation instruction. SQL produces rule-level failures and entity-level scores, while the BI layer summarizes quality by source, country, domain, and reporting period.

Example workflow

Source profiling
    ↓

Dashboard outputs

  • Overall quality score and trend.
  • Failures by source and quality dimension.
  • High-severity remediation queue.
  • Duplicate and unresolved identity analysis.
  • Before-and-after remediation comparison.

Business value

The framework moves data quality from ad hoc investigation to a measurable operating process. Teams can prioritize the failures with the greatest downstream impact and confirm whether remediation work improves the quality score over time.
