Executive KPI and WBR Framework

A repeatable reporting system that connects governed metric definitions, automated datasets, weekly trends, variance commentary, and accountable follow-up actions.

SQL, Power BI, TableauPrimary tools
Senior leadershipAudience
Weekly business reviewCadence

The challenge

Weekly reporting often becomes a manual exercise in reconciling numbers from different teams. Leaders may receive the same KPI with different definitions, refresh dates, filters, or denominators, reducing trust and leaving less time for decision-making.

Framework design

  1. Define each KPI in business language.
  2. Document numerator, denominator, grain, exclusions, owner, and refresh timing.
  3. Build a governed SQL or semantic-layer model.
  4. Validate totals against source control reports.
  5. Automate the dashboard refresh and variance inputs.
  6. Capture actions, owners, and due dates from the review.

WBR experience

The dashboard is organized around current performance, historical trend, target variance, major drivers, risks, and next actions. Commentary is focused on what changed and why, rather than restating the values already visible in the chart.

Quality controls

  • Freshness status displayed with each reporting period.
  • Source-to-dashboard reconciliation checks.
  • Automated duplicate and null monitoring.
  • Version-controlled metric definitions.
  • Named business and technical owners.

Business value

The framework reduces debate about whose number is correct and shifts the review toward root causes, tradeoffs, and decisions. It also makes onboarding easier because metric logic and ownership are documented rather than held by individual analysts.
